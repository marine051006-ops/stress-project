Human Stress Detector

Using advanced machine learning, we analyze your physiological signals to provide real-time stress detection. Understand your body, manage your stress, and find support when you need it most.

How It Works

A simple, three-step process to understand your well-being.

1. Collect Signals

The system acquires physiological signals from sensors like ECG, GSR, and Body Temperature.

2. Analyze Features

A Decision Tree Classifier analyzes key features extracted from the signals to identify stress patterns.

3. Output Stress Level

Receive a clear classification, from "Calm" to "Very High Stress," helping you understand your state.

How It Works

This stress detection system operates through a user-friendly web interface shown on the Detection Page. Instead of relying on live sensors, this application empowers you to manually input your current physiological data using a series of simple sliders.

You provide values for eight key parameters: GSR Data, Respiration Rate, Body Temperature, Limb Movement, Blood Oxygen, Rapid Eye Movement, Sleeping Hour, and Heart Rate.

Once you have entered these values, the system feeds this into its core: a pre-trained Decision Tree Classifier. (In this static version, this logic is simulated). This machine learning model analyzes the specific combination of your inputs, comparing them to patterns it learned during its training.

Based on this analysis, the model immediately provides a "Stress level detected..." message, classifying your current state into one of four distinct levels: Stress Free and Calm, Low Stress, Medium Stress, or High Stress.
